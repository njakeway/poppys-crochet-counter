Pattern

Paste your pattern with its row headings, such as “Row 3” or “3rd Row”. The app will show what to do next based on your row and stitch counts.

Yarn colours

Add each yarn name as it appears in the pattern and pick a matching colour. The counter then shows which yarn you are using and can colour the big button to match.

Backup

Copy your backup into a note or message. Paste it here later to get your project back.

Row 1 is at the bottom, and rows flip direction each time you turn the work. Bright squares are done, faded squares are still to do, and the square with a ring is your next stitch.
